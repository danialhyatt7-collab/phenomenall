    Chest

    Armpit to armpit across the front, doubled.

    Length

    Highest point of the shoulder straight down to the hem.

    Shoulder

    Seam to seam across the back. On a drop shoulder these seams sit below your actual shoulder.

    Sleeve

    Shoulder seam down to the sleeve opening.
